# Basel's Research Hub

Create a premium, institutional-quality personal finance portfolio and investment research website for:

Basel M. Alghamdi
Finance Student @ KAU

The website will serve as my professional portfolio and personal research publication platform. I will use it when applying for internships and finance opportunities, and I may link it on my CV and LinkedIn.

The website should allow me to continuously publish:

Investment research articles

Equity research

Company analysis

Investment theses

Industry research

Macro and market commentary

Financial modeling projects

Valuation projects

University finance projects

PDF research reports

Excel financial models

The website should feel like a small institutional research platform rather than a typical student portfolio or personal blog.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a sophisticated, understated institutional-finance aesthetic inspired by the visual discipline of major financial institutions such as Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, BlackRock, and Citi.

Do NOT copy any company's website, branding, logo, typography, or exact color palette.

Instead, capture the general qualities associated with institutional financial research:

Professional

Analytical

Conservative

Premium

Credible

Minimal

Data-focused

Editorial

Timeless

Avoid anything that looks like:

An AI-generated startup website

A crypto website

A fintech landing page

A flashy student portfolio

Excessive gradients

Glassmorphism

Neon colors

Oversized rounded cards

Excessive animations

Generic AI-generated illustrations

The website should look credible if viewed by an analyst, portfolio manager, investment banker, recruiter, or finance professor.

COLOR SYSTEM

Use a restrained institutional palette.

Primary:

Deep navy

Near-black navy

White

Off-white

Charcoal

Light institutional gray
